About this test

Ballotta is an early test for Travis County voters only. It isn't affiliated with Travis County, the League of Women Voters, or any campaign, and it doesn't tell you how to vote.

Districts: Travis County Tax Office voter registration data (public map server). State and federal districts: Texas Legislative Council, CC BY 4.0. Contests and candidates: Travis County's consolidated ballot for Nov 3, 2026 (corrected Oct 14). Candidate answers and the campaign websites we link: the League of Women Voters of the Austin Area 2026 Voters Guide, linked as published. Voting locations: Travis County Clerk; map positions from the U.S. Census geocoder, Travis County and © OpenStreetMap contributors. Contests marked "Double-check" aren't confirmed yet; one district on the county ballot (Travis County MUD No. 41) can't be placed on a map yet. Always compare with your official ballot using the county’s Voter Lookup (search by name and birth date).
