What’s going on here?

What is Ballotta?

A free, independent tool for Travis County voters. Enter your address, see every race and proposition on your Nov 3 ballot, and work through them one at a time.

Does it tell me how to vote?

No. Ballotta doesn’t endorse, rank or recommend anyone. Every candidate gets the same links, and we say so when something’s missing.

Where does the information come from?

Travis County’s own ballot and district data, the League of Women Voters of the Austin Area voters guide, and official sources for each measure. We link to them rather than rewrite them.

What happens to my address and picks?

Your address goes from your browser to Travis County’s map server (and, if the county can’t match it, the U.S. Census geocoder); we never receive it. Picks and reasons stay in your browser unless you send yourself or a friend a link.

What won’t you allow here?

No ads, no paid placement, no campaign content, and no selling or sharing data. We try to block share names that pretend to be the League, the county or a party. We count anonymous taps, never who you are.

Is this official?

No. Ballotta isn’t affiliated with Travis County, the League or any campaign. Check your official sample ballot at VoteTravis.gov before you vote.

About this test

Ballotta is an early test for Travis County voters only. It isn't affiliated with Travis County, the League of Women Voters, or any campaign, and it doesn't tell you how to vote.
