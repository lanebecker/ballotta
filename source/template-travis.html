What’s going on here?

What is Ballotta?

A free, independent tool for Travis County voters. Enter your address, see every race and proposition on your Nov 3 ballot, and work through them one at a time.

Does it tell me how to vote?

No. Ballotta doesn’t endorse, rank or recommend anyone. Every candidate gets the same links, and we say so when something’s missing.

Where does the information come from?

Travis County’s own ballot and district data, the League of Women Voters of the Austin Area voters guide, and official sources for each measure. We link to them rather than rewrite them.

What happens to my address and picks?

Your address goes from your browser to Travis County’s map server (and, if the county can’t match it, the U.S. Census geocoder); we never receive it. Picks and reasons stay in your browser unless you send yourself or a friend a link. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, we count nothing at all.

What won’t you allow here?

No ads, no paid placement, no campaign content, and no selling or sharing data. We try to block share names that pretend to be the League, the county or a party. We count anonymous taps, never who you are.

Is this official?

No. Ballotta isn’t affiliated with Travis County, the League or any campaign. Check your official sample ballot at VoteTravis.gov before you vote.

About this test

Ballotta is an early test for Travis County voters only. It isn't affiliated with Travis County, the League of Women Voters, or any campaign, and it doesn't tell you how to vote.

Districts: Travis County Tax Office voter registration data (public map server). State and federal districts: Texas Legislative Council, CC BY 4.0. Candidate answers and the campaign websites we link: the League of Women Voters of the Austin Area 2026 Voters Guide, linked as published. Add Ballotta to your Home Screen

It opens like an app, and your phone is less likely to clear your picks.

  1. Copy your picks. ✓ Copied The Home Screen app starts empty, so bring a private link to your picks.
    This is the same private link as Keep my picks, not the Share my picks link for friends. Anyone with it can see your picks and reasons, so paste it only into your own Ballotta.
  2. Add the icon with Safari’s buttons, not Ballotta’s. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. Don’t see it? Tap View More first, and it’s in that list. Then tap Add. You can leave this window open. Don’t see Add to Home Screen at all? If another app opened this page, tap its compass or Open in Safari first.
  3. Open Ballotta from your Home Screen and tap Paste my picks link at the top. Your iPhone shows a small Paste bubble; tap it.
