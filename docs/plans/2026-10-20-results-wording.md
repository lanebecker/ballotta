# Results wording — first review pass (draft for Lane, Oct 20)

Every visitor-facing string the results feature adds, numbered for review. Tone (Lane, Oct 20): "Didn't win", never
"Lost"; no ✓ or ✗ marks; "Leading", not "Ahead", and never "winning" before official; "Unofficial" on every ribbon.
Names, counties, dates and times below are placeholders. Separators and quotes follow the page (· and curly quotes).

## A. The status line (top of every ribbon)

- **W1** Unofficial, one source: "Unofficial · Travis County, posted Nov 4, 8:41 am"
- **W2** Unofficial, several counties: "Unofficial · Travis County, posted Nov 4, 8:41 am · Williamson County, Nov 4,
  7:55 am · Hays County, Nov 4, 7:15 am"
- **W3** Unofficial, state race: "Unofficial · Texas Secretary of State, posted Nov 4, 8:41 am"
- **W4** Under every unofficial status, through Nov 17: "Late mail, overseas and provisional ballots are still being
  counted."
- **W5** Under every unofficial status, after Nov 17 (state races): "Counting is finished in every county. The result
  becomes official at the state canvass."
- **W6** "Official by", before a date is announced: "Becomes official when the Austin City Council canvasses the vote,
  by Nov 17."
- **W7** "Official by", date announced: "Becomes official when the Austin City Council canvasses the vote on Nov 12."
- **W8** "Official by", state: "Becomes official at the state canvass, Nov 21–Dec 7."
- **W9** "Official by", Governor and Lt. Governor: "Becomes official when the Legislature canvasses the vote, after it
  meets Jan 12, 2027."
- **W10** Official: "Official · canvassed by the Austin City Council, Nov 12"
- **W11** Official, numbers not published: "Official · canvassed by the state, Dec 2 · numbers are the last unofficial

- **W13** No votes yet: "No votes reported yet"

## H. Explainers

- **W55** Link from W46: "Why unofficial?" → Feedback & Info, "What’s a canvass?":

  > Election night counts are unofficial. For about two weeks after Election Day, counties keep counting late mail
  > ballots, ballots from overseas voters and the military, and provisional ballots once voters have confirmed them.
  > Then the body that called each election (the county, a city council, a school board, a utility district’s board)
  > meets to “canvass” the vote: it reviews the count and declares the result official. State and federal races are
  > canvassed by the state between Nov 21 and Dec 7. The Governor and Lieutenant Governor races are canvassed by the
  > Legislature in January. Ballotta shows each result as unofficial until its canvass, then marks it official.

- **W56** Feedback & Info, "Where do results come from?":

  > From each county’s published results (Travis, Williamson, Bastrop and Hays) and the Texas Secretary of State, added
  > up for contests that cross county lines. Ballotta updates them once or twice a day. It doesn’t call races: a
  > “leader” is only who’s ahead in the count so far.

- **W57** About this test credit: "Results: Texas Secretary of State; Travis, Williamson, Bastrop, Hays, Caldwell and
  Lee counties. Unofficial until each canvass. Ballotta doesn’t call races."
